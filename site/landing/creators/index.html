For Creators

Give fans a better game-day conversation

SportsClaw is a personal sports AI agent in Telegram and WhatsApp. Fans tell it their teams, then ask about games, lineups and news and follow up in the same chat. If you make game-day content, here is how to show it to your audience honestly.

Applications aren’t open.

There is no creator program to join yet, and nothing on this page is an offer. It's guidance for creators who already want to show SportsClaw in their own content.

Three game-day formats

Each one starts with a real question your audience would ask, and the follow-up is where the conversation gets interesting.

  • Before the game

    Pregame

    Pick the matchup your audience cares about. Ask what decides it, then follow up on the part your fans argue about most.

    Try askingWhat's the key matchup for my team, and what would change it?

  • Lineup lock

    Fantasy

    Share your league's scoring and the two players you're stuck between. Show the call, the reasoning and what would flip it.

    Try askingHalf-PPR, one flex spot. Which of these two, and why?

  • After the whistle

    Postgame

    Talk through what happened with your audience's takes in mind. Ask what held up, what didn't and what to watch next time.

    Try askingWhat actually swung that game for my team?

Start from a public example

The guide has a curated historical example from a published NBA recap: the result, then what changed. Then ask the same about your own team in your own chat before you show anything.

Show the real product. Say how you're involved.

Real product

  • Use the actual Telegram or WhatsApp chat, with your own questions.
  • Don't rewrite answers, stitch replies together or present a scripted exchange as a live one. If you trim for length, say so.
  • Check scores, stats and news before you repeat them. Data can lag, and answers can be wrong.
  • Don't describe features you haven't seen work in your own chat.

Disclosure

  • If you have any connection to SportsClaw, say so clearly in the content itself, not only in a profile or link.
  • Follow the disclosure rules of your platform and where you publish.
  • SportsClaw explains market information; it doesn't place bets or promise winners. Don't present an answer as a guaranteed pick.
  • Keep betting and market talk within your platform's rules and away from audiences it isn't meant for.
