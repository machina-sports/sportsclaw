Privacy

Draft for review. Not yet in effect. This page is still being reviewed and has no effective date. It doesn't govern SportsClaw yet and may change before it does.

Who we are

SportsClaw is built by Machina Sports and operated by GeniusTech Corporation, a Delaware corporation. In this notice, “we” and “us” mean GeniusTech Corporation.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the hosted SportsClaw service: this website and the official SportsClaw chats in Telegram and WhatsApp linked from it.

It doesn't cover copies of the open-source agent that you or anyone else host. Whoever runs that copy decides how it handles data. It also doesn't cover any future native app, which would come with its own disclosures. Telegram, WhatsApp and Stripe handle data under their own policies.

What we collect

When you visit this website. Like any web server, ours receives ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser details, the page you asked for, the page that sent you and the time of the request. If you use the light/dark switch, the page saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it's remembered next time. That choice stays in your browser.

When you use the chat.

  • Your messaging account identifiers, as applicable: your Telegram user and chat IDs, or your WhatsApp phone number.
  • What you send: your messages and questions, and supported uploads such as images.
  • Chat context and preferences we store so the agent can pick up where you left off, such as your teams, your fantasy league's scoring, takes you save and how much detail you like.
  • Usage and diagnostic information, such as when and how the service is used, errors and performance.

When you subscribe. Stripe hosts the checkout and processes the payment. We receive your plan and the status of your payments and subscription, with the related Stripe identifiers. We don't receive your full card number.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and keep the context of your chat.
  • To run your plan, including subscriptions and payment status.
  • To keep the service working and safe, including finding and fixing problems and preventing abuse.
  • To improve the service.

Who else receives it

  • Messaging platforms. Telegram and WhatsApp carry your messages and keep their own copies under their own policies.
  • Infrastructure providers that host and run the service.
  • AI model providers that process your messages and the relevant chat context to generate replies.
  • Stripe, for checkout, payments and subscription management.

Each of them may receive the information relevant to the work it does for the service.

How long we keep it

We keep chat context and preferences so the agent can remember them. This draft doesn't set fixed retention periods.

Your requests

To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, email support@machina.gg. We may need to confirm the request comes from you, for example from the same chat account, before acting on it.

  • Deleting your data doesn't cancel a subscription, and cancelling with /billing doesn't delete your data. If you want both, ask for both.
  • Copies of your messages kept by Telegram or WhatsApp are managed in those apps, not by us.

You may have further rights under the law where you live.

Changes and contact

When this notice takes effect, its date will appear on this page, and we'll update it if our practices change. Questions about this notice: support@machina.gg.
