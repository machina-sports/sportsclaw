Who can use SportsClaw (proposed)

You would need to be at least 18 and allowed to use SportsClaw under the law where you live.

What answers are, and aren't

SportsClaw is an AI agent. Its answers can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, and sports data can lag. Check anything that matters to you, especially scores, injuries, lineups and prices, before you rely on it. Answers are general information, not professional, financial, legal or betting advice.

Markets are read-only

SportsClaw can read and explain public market information. It doesn't place bets or trades, take deposits, hold money or access your accounts or wallets, and it doesn't promise any result. Any decision to trade or bet is yours, under the market's own rules and the law where you are.

Other platforms

Plans and billing in the chat

This section covers subscriptions started from the website or the official chats, which are paid through Stripe.

  • Free includes a daily usage allowance. It isn't a trial: it doesn't turn into a paid plan on its own.
  • Pro is a recurring paid subscription. Send /upgrade in the chat to start. Stripe's checkout shows the price and how often you're billed before you agree to pay.
  • A subscription renews each billing period until you cancel. Send /billing in the chat to open subscription management, where it's available. Cancelling stops future renewals.
  • This draft doesn't set a refund policy.

If an iPhone app is released (proposed, not active)

These terms don't cover native app operations. The following billing provisions are proposed for review and would apply only if the iPhone app offers a subscription through Apple.

  • A subscription purchased through Apple would be billed to your Apple Account. The app would show its price and billing period before you buy.
  • It would renew automatically until you cancel. You would manage or cancel it in your Apple account's subscription settings, and cancelling would stop future renewals.
  • Refund requests for Apple purchases would go to Apple. The consumer rights below still apply.
  • Manage or cancel each subscription through the channel where you bought it.

The read-only market limits above would also apply to the app. Apple's standard end user license agreement would apply to the app separately from these service terms.
