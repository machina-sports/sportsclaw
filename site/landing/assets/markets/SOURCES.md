# Market wordmarks

These marks identify market coverage, not sponsorship, endorsement or partnership. Trademark rights remain with their owners.

- `novig-wordmark.svg`: exact inline wordmark from the public official [Novig widgets page](https://widgets.novig.com/), extracted from `div.logo-word`. Transparent white paths; original geometry unchanged.
- `prophetx-wordmark.svg`: inline header `svg[data-slot=logo-mark]` from [ProphetX](https://www.prophetx.co/). Original geometry unchanged. The `var(--primary)` fill is resolved to `currentColor` so the X renders when the SVG is loaded as a standalone image, without depending on host-page CSS variables.
- `polymarket-wordmark.svg`: unmodified `logo-black.svg` from the [official logo archive](https://polymarket-upload.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/polymarket-logos.zip), linked by [Polymarket's brand page](https://polymarket.com/brand).
- `kalshi-wordmark.svg`: unmodified [2026 SVG hosted by Wikimedia Commons](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b8/Kalshi-logo-2026.svg). The [file's attribution page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kalshi-logo-2026.svg) credits Kalshi Inc. and cites [Kalshi's brand kit](https://kalshi.com/brandkit). The primary brand/CDN fetches were blocked, so this is a documented mirror, not a directly downloaded primary asset.

The landing page displays monochrome black or white through CSS filters according to theme. Source geometry and aspect ratios remain intact. Transparent SVGs avoid the baked-in pale backgrounds in the older PNGs. Source files contain no scripts or external resource references.
