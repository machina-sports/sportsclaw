Questions before kickoff

What is SportsClaw?

Your personal sports agent, in Telegram or WhatsApp. Tell it your teams, your league and what you're watching, then ask about a lineup, a game, the news or a market price. It looks things up, ties them to what you've told it and gives you a straight take. There's no app to install: tap a chat button on this page and start free.

What does it remember?

Within the same chat, it keeps the conversation, the details you share about yourself (teams, leagues, scoring, how much detail you like) and any takes you ask it to save. Telegram and WhatsApp are separate chats, so what you tell it in one stays there. It replies when you message it; it doesn't watch games in the background or send you alerts on its own.

Which sports and markets does it cover?

The sports in the coverage list, plus general sports news. For prediction markets it reads public market information from Novig, ProphetX, Kalshi and Polymarket: send the market you're looking at and it explains the price and the contract rules. Detail varies by sport, competition and market.

Where does the information come from?

Its sports-data tools look up scores, schedules, stats and news, and it reads public market information when it's available. It keeps what it found apart from its own take, and tells you when something couldn't be confirmed or may be out of date.

Can it help with fantasy?

Yes, from what you tell it. Share the players, your league's scoring and any notes, and it compares them and makes a call. It doesn't log in to your fantasy league.

Will it place bets or promise winners?

No. It reads market information and explains it, and you make the call. It never places bets or trades, has no access to your wallet or accounts, and no answer comes with a guaranteed result.

Is it free?

Yes. Open Telegram or WhatsApp and start chatting. See the plan cards for what's included.

How do plans, upgrades and cancelling work?

Everyone starts on Free. Send /upgrade for Pro or /upgrade max for Max to see available upgrade options. If you're subscribed, send /billing to manage or cancel your subscription.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. The chat on this page is the hosted version: we run it, and you start free in Telegram or WhatsApp. To run your own, you host the agent and connect your own LLM, and those hosting and model costs are yours. Explore open source.
