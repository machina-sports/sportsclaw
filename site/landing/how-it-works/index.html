How it works

Ask about your team. Then ask what changed.

SportsClaw is a personal sports AI agent in Telegram and WhatsApp. Open a chat, ask a real question about a game, a lineup or the news, and keep following up in the same chat.

A good first question

Start with a game you care about. Any of these works as a first message; copy one into your chat.

  • After a gameWhat changed in my team's last game? Ask me which team first.
  • Before a gameWhat's the key matchup in my team's next game, and what would change it?
  • Set it upRemember my teams and keep your answers short. Ask me who I follow.

Ask the same about your own team

Swap in your team and its most recent game: ask who won, then ask what changed. SportsClaw looks up scores, stats and news with its sports-data tools, tries to keep what it found apart from its own take, and says when something couldn't be confirmed. Data can lag and answers can be wrong, so check anything time-sensitive.

Straight answers

Does it remember what I said earlier?
Yes, within the same chat. It keeps the conversation, the details you share (teams, leagues, how much detail you like) and notes you ask it to save. Telegram and WhatsApp are separate chats, so what you tell it in one stays there.
Do I need to create or sync an account?
No. You start in the Telegram or WhatsApp app you already use. There's nothing to install and no account, fantasy league or wallet to connect or sync.
Does it place bets?
No. It can read and explain public market information if you ask, but it never places bets or trades and no answer comes with a guaranteed result.
Is it free?
Free to start, with a daily usage allowance. Pro gives you more room; see the plans on the homepage.
